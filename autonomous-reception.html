Dual Production Architectures

Selecting the Right Voice Stack for Your Business

Depending on whether your primary priority is sub-400ms native audio speed or deep multi-system agentic reasoning with persistent caller memory, we deploy one of two hardened telephony pipelines.

01 • GOOGLE GEMINI DIRECT SOLUTION
Native Multimodal Voice Stream

Ultra-Low Latency & Cost Efficiency

Designed for maximum speed and cost efficiency. Instead of stitching together separate transcription and voice generation tools, this architecture streams caller audio directly from Twilio into Google's Gemini Multimodal Live API, which listens, reasons, and speaks natively in a single pass.

[Caller] ⇄ [Twilio Voice] ⇄ [Node.js/Python Bridge] ⇄ [Gemini Live API] ⇄ [Booking REST API]
  • Ultra-Low Latency (~300ms–500ms): Native audio-to-audio processing eliminates the traditional Speech-to-Text (STT) and Text-to-Speech (TTS) bottleneck.
  • Natural Interruption (Barge-In): If a caller changes their mind mid-sentence, Gemini stops speaking immediately and adapts on the fly.
  • Simplified Maintenance: Fewer external API keys and moving parts reduce failure points and lower per-minute operating costs.
02 • OPENCLAW + CLAUDE 4.6 SOLUTION
Modular Agentic Pipeline Integration

Deep Reasoning, Custom Voices & Persistent Memory

Designed for complex business logic, bespoke brand voices, and omnichannel memory. This modular pipeline pairs best-in-class speech engines with Anthropic's Claude 4.6 reasoning models orchestrated inside an OpenClaw agent gateway.

[Caller] ⇄ [Twilio] ⇄ [Voice Gateway (Deepgram STT + ElevenLabs TTS)] ⇄ [OpenClaw + Claude 4.6] ⇄ [Booking API]
  • Frontier Reasoning Engine: Uses Claude 4.6 Haiku for rapid turn-taking or Claude 4.6 Sonnet for complex multi-step scheduling rules and policy edge cases.
  • Bespoke Brand Voices: Integrates with ElevenLabs or Cartesia for hyper-realistic, custom-cloned voices tailored to your brand identity.
  • Omnichannel Caller Memory: OpenClaw maintains persistent state across phone calls, SMS follow-ups, and internal Slack/Teams escalations.

Production Readiness

Deployment Prerequisites Checklist

What our engineering team configures alongside your stakeholders to take an autonomous voice receptionist from sandbox to live telephony.

STEP 01

Telephony & SIP Provisioning

Dedicated Twilio phone number provisioning (or SIP trunking/call-forwarding from your existing main business line) with low-latency regional media routing.

STEP 02

API Credentials & Webhooks

Scoped API keys for your calendar/CRM platform (e.g., HubSpot, Salesforce, HighLevel, Square, ServiceTitan, or custom REST APIs) plus LLM provider keys.

STEP 03

Guardrails & Warm Escalation

Business hours configuration, custom system prompts, edge-case fallback rules, and instant warm-transfer routing to human staff for high-priority calls.
