Why We Went 100% Organic

Inspired by Traditional Korean Care for Natural Fibers

In Korean culture, fine textiles like raw silk (명주) and ramie linen (모시) are treated as living heirlooms passed down through generations. When my wife Ji-Hyun and I took over our first plant in Maryland, we saw firsthand how conventional petrochemical dry-cleaning solvents brittle natural fibers and leave a lingering chemical odor on clothing worn against your skin.

We invested in European biodegradable wet-cleaning lagoons and organic botanical hydrocarbon systems. Your clothes return smelling like fresh mountain air, with vibrant colors and zero toxic residue in our Chesapeake Bay watershed.

Our Three-Step Quality Guarantee

How Every Garment Is Treated Inside Our Studio

We never batch-process your wardrobe with harsh industrial heat. Every piece follows our signature three-step protocol.

1. Ultrasonic & Botanical Pre-Spotting

Before cleaning begins, Ji-Hyun inspects every garment under daylight-balanced LED lamps, using cold ultrasonic mist and plant-derived enzymes to lift collar oils, coffee, and wine without bleaching fabric dye.

Fiber-Safe Chemistry Zero Dye Stripping

2. Moisture-Sensing Lagoon Care

Our computer-monitored cylinders gently cradle wool, silk, and cashmere in biodegradable conditioners, controlling residual moisture to the exact percentage so fibers never shrink or felt.

Lanolin-Restoring Softness Guaranteed

3. Hand-Tension Steam Finishing

Every jacket, dress, and trouser is shaped on breathable wooden forms and hand-finished with soft-plate irons. Missing or cracked buttons are replaced free of charge before final inspection.

Free Button Repair Hand-Inspected
