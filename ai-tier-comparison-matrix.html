TIER 2 • THE ENGINE ROOM

The API Layer

Cost Structure Analysis

Pay-as-you-go token and audio-minute consumption + initial systems engineering. Highly capital-efficient—you pay strictly for actual automated work executed.

TIER 3 • THE VAULT

Sovereign & Self-Contained AI

Cost Structure Analysis

Significant upfront CapEx (dedicated enterprise GPUs or reserved cloud clusters) paired with predictable long-term OpEx and zero per-token vendor fees at massive volume.

TIER 2 • FRONTIER PROPRIETARY APIS

When Maximum Reasoning & Multimodal Speed Matter

  • GPT-5.4: Industry benchmark for complex structured output, deep multi-step planning, and broad tool-use reliability.
  • Gemini 3.1 Pro & Live: Unmatched 2M+ token context window and sub-400ms native audio/video streaming for real-time telephony and media pipelines.
  • Claude 4.6 (Sonnet / Opus): Superior agentic coding, nuanced customer communication, and deterministic adherence to complex system guardrails inside OpenClaw.
TIER 3 • SOVEREIGN OPEN-WEIGHT MODELS

When Data Custody & Zero Token Fees Matter

  • Llama 4: Versatile multimodal open-weight backbone ideal for private RAG knowledge bases and fine-tuned enterprise adapters (LoRA).
  • DeepSeek V4: Exceptional mathematical, analytical, and reasoning density at a fraction of traditional compute overhead.
  • Qwen 3.5: Top-tier multilingual, coding, and structured tool-calling performance for self-hosted agentic clusters.
