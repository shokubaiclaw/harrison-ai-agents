When Data Becomes the Program

Three existential dilemmas emerge that legacy cybersecurity tools cannot solve:

  • 01. Gradient Descent Trojans MCAs no longer need buffer overflows or stolen admin credentials to plant a backdoor—they seed poisoned records into open web or collaborative training pools so your own gradient descent pipeline compiles their trojan directly into your model weights.
  • 02. Model Inversion & Reconstruction Your model weights act as a compressed, queryable archive of sensitive training records, allowing MCAs with standard API access to reconstruct private data through membership inference and model inversion.
  • 03. The Unlearning Dilemma When a regulator, customer, or incident response team demands permanent removal of a trained record, you cannot run a SQL delete query against a 10B-parameter tensor where every sample entangles across the entire network.
