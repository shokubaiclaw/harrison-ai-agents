Our Local Story

Created as an Antidote to Rushed Strip-Mall Salons

Arundel Luxe Nail Studio was founded with a straightforward promise: local clients shouldn't have to choose between convenient neighborhood walk-in hours and high-end boutique quality.

Many traditional salons rush clients through chairs using harsh high-speed drills, thin out natural nail beds, and leave a heavy chemical smell in the air. We redesigned every part of the studio experience around gentle natural nail preservation, source-capture ventilation, and transparent pricing.

Three-Point Safety Protocol

Our Uncompromising Clinical Standard

Your health and comfort come first. Here is what happens behind the scenes before every single appointment.

1. Sealed Autoclave Sterilization

All stainless steel nippers, pushers, and diamond bits are scrubbed, placed in an ultrasonic enzymatic bath, and heat-sterilized in sealed medical pouches that are opened in front of you.

Spore-Tested Weekly Medical Grade

2. 100% Single-Use Disposables

We never reuse porous nail files, buffer blocks, pumice pads, or toe separators. Your disposable kit is brand-new for your service and yours to take home or recycle afterward.

Zero Cross-Use Fresh Every Guest

3. Pure-Air Ventilation & Non-Toxic Gels

Our tables feature built-in dust collectors and activated carbon filtration, paired with 10-Free, cruelty-free, and HEMA-free Japanese and Korean gel systems.

Allergy-Conscious Odor-Controlled
