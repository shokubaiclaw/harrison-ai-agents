Data Protection & Privacy Standards

PRIVACY POLICY & AI DATA GOVERNANCE

Effective Year: 2026 • Harrison AI Agents (a division of the Harrison Arbitrage LLC brand family). We engineer every web platform, paid media funnel, voice receptionist, and LLM workflow with strict data minimization and cryptographic security.

CLAUSE 01

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information required to scope, deliver, and optimize our technical services:

  • Direct Business Inquiries: Name, organization, work email, phone number, and project specifications submitted via our contact and onboarding forms.
  • Technical Telemetry: Non-invasive browser preferences (such as local Light/Dark theme state), IP-level security logs, and aggregate site performance metrics.
  • Client System Credentials: Scoped API keys and webhook tokens provided during onboarding, stored strictly in encrypted secret vaults.
CLAUSE 02

2. Paid Media & Attribution Telemetry

When executing AI-Driven Ad Campaigns on behalf of our clients or our own platform, we utilize privacy-compliant attribution protocols:

  • Click & Conversion Identifiers: UTM parameters, Google Click IDs (gclid), and Meta/LinkedIn click identifiers used to measure return on ad spend (ROAS).
  • Server-Side Conversion APIs (CAPI): Hashed conversion events (using SHA-256) transmitted server-to-server to ad platforms strictly to optimize campaign delivery in compliance with platform privacy terms.
CLAUSE 03

3. Voice Telephony & Booking Data Processing

Our 24/7 autonomous reception and smart scheduling agents process live audio streams and appointment payloads strictly as a data processor on behalf of the deploying enterprise:

  • Real-Time Audio Streams: Inbound and outbound voice streams routed via Twilio SIP/WebSockets are processed in real time to execute caller requests and calendar bookings.
  • No Unauthorized Voice Cloning: Caller audio is never harvested to train public foundation models or synthesize unauthorized voice clones.
CLAUSE 04

4. AI Model Tiers & Zero-Data-Retention (ZDR)

We architect AI workflows to prevent proprietary business logic or customer PII from leaking into public training corpora:

  • Tier 2 Commercial APIs: Workloads routed through OpenAI, Google Cloud/Vertex, or Anthropic APIs utilize commercial API endpoints governed by enterprise Zero-Data-Retention (ZDR) and no-training policies where applicable.
  • Tier 3 Sovereign Vaults: For regulated industries, we deploy self-contained open-weight models (Llama 4, DeepSeek V4, Qwen 3.5) inside client-controlled VPCs or on-premise hardware with zero external network egress.
CLAUSE 05

5. Data Security & Third-Party Subprocessors

We do not sell, rent, or broker personal or enterprise data to third parties. Data is shared only with vetted infrastructure subprocessors necessary to operate your selected architecture (such as cloud hosting, Twilio telephony carriers, and your designated CRM/calendar endpoints), all bound by strict TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest.
