PRIVACY POLICY.

Data Protection & Privacy Standards

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with Harrison Agents, we collect personal information you voluntarily provide through our contact forms or direct communications, such as your name, email address, and project specifications. We also automatically collect non-personal technical data including IP addresses, browser types, and usage metrics to optimize site performance.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly utilized to deliver, manage, and optimize our AI agent services, respond to inquiries, and process service engagements. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. AI Architecture & Data Security

Client data and operational parameters shared during custom agent deployments are protected using industry-standard encryption protocols. Proprietary information processed through our agent workflows remains strictly confidential and is handled in accordance with enterprise-grade data protection guidelines.

4. Third-Party Integration

Our website and agent solutions may interface with trusted third-party providers (such as hosting infrastructure and domain providers). These providers have independent privacy policies governing data managed on their platforms.

5. Contact & Data Inquiries

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request data deletion or modification, please contact us directly via our contact page or email us at info@harrisonaiagents.com.
